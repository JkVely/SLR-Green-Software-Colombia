# Desarrollo de estudio técnico-económico para mejorar la eficiencia energética aplicada a centros de datos TIER II en Colombia
## Abstract
> In the project, a technical-economic study will be carried out to improve energy efficiency for a TIER II level data center endorsed by the Uptime Institute _the most prestigious and worldwide recognition in the creation and administration of TIER standards and certifications for the design, construction, and operation of data centers_, analyzing each of the factors that can lead to be relevant and look for a mechanism that allows improving energy savings in the data center, starting with of a technical information survey of each of the subsystems that make up the data center. Subsequently, an analysis of the information obtained will be carried out in which a categorization of the electrical energy consumption for each subsystem and a classification of technologies where, through technical sheets will verify the operating conditions of the infrastructure to finally address a stage of preparation of a technical-economic proposal where a series of recommendations will be issued that contemplate from technological migrations in the equipment to a possible energy implementation photovoltaic solar as a source of energy supply for some of the datacenter subsystems.
