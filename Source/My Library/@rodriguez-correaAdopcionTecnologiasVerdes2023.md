# Adopción de Tecnologías Verdes en el Sector Industrial: una Revisión Sistemática de la Literatura.
## Abstract
> Green technologies have been consolidating as a contribution to the sustainable development of the industrial sector_ however, little is known about the factors that affect the adoption of these technologies in this sector. Given this need, the objective of examining the factors associated with the adoption of green technologies in the individual and business context arises. The methodology was structured from a systematic review that follows the steps of the PRISMA methodology, from the search in the Scopus, Web of Science and Dimensions databases. The analysis of 123 documents was obtained as a result. Among the findings, it is observed that the relevant factors are the intention of use, social norms, attitude, and ecological awareness. It is concluded that the validation of these factors is necessary in the context of emerging economies, to establish strategies that respond to the needs of their social, technological, and economic context.

## Notas clave
- Revisión sistemática _basada en PRISMA_ sobre la adopción de tecnologías verdes en el sector industrial. Analiza 123 documentos de Scopus, Web of Science y Dimensions.
- Factores determinantes de la adopción: Intención de uso, normas sociales, actitud y conciencia ecológica.
- Conclusión clave: Existe una necesidad urgente de validar estos factores específicamente en economías emergentes para crear estrategias que respondan al contexto socioeconómico local.
- Relevancia para el proyecto: Valida el uso de la metodología PRISMA para este tema y refuerza la tesis de que Colombia necesita estrategias de adopción adaptadas a su contexto.
- Uso sugerido: Sección de Metodología _justificar PRISMA_ y Resultados _factores de adopción_.
