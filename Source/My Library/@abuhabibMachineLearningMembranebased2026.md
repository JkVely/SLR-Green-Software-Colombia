# Machine learning in membrane-based desalination and wastewater treatment: An AI-assisted bibliometric analysis
## Abstract
> This study aims to investigate the research landscape, thematic evolution, and emerging trends in the integration of machine learning with membrane-based desalination and wastewater treatment technologies through an AI-assisted bibliometric analysis. This study conducts an AI assisted bibliometric analysis of 1195 publications for the period (2005–2024) from Scopus scholarly database to identify recent research trends in the field of water desalination and wastewater treatment integrated with machine learning. With the application of PRISMA screening employing ChatGPT and Gemini for documents analysis and VoSviewer visualization, three thematic clusters emerged from co-occurrence keywords perspectives, namely, conventional membrane and wastewater studies, machine learning and predictive modelling, desalination and energy integrated systems. The specific objectives of this analysis are: 1) Quantitatively map global research targeting desalination and wastewater applications using membrane technology integrated with ML 2) Identify publication trends, leading contributors, and influential research themes 3) Reveal emerging directions through keyword evolution and thematic analysis, and 4) Explore network or interrelationships globally. The PRISMA screening following the inclusion criteria resulted in 498 relevant articles which in turn were analyzed and investigated by VoSviewer accordingly. From country collaboration perspective, China is the globally leading country followed by India and Saudi Arabia in both number of publications and citations. In addition, the study revealed a strong connection between desalination, solar energy, and energy efficiency illustrating interdisciplinary collaboration among environmental engineering, computer science, and renewable energy research. The evolution pattern indicates a transition from mechanical modelling to data-driven hybrid frameworks, highlighting automation, sustainability, and intelligent decision-making. Overall, future progress will rely on coordinated data sharing, strengthened collaborative networks, and targeted dataset expansion to enable robust, transparent, and optimized ML-driven membrane technologies. © 2026 The Author(s).
