# Model for the Intent to Adopt Green IT in the Context of Organizations
## Abstract
> In organizations, part of sustainability is manifested through green information technology (GIT) for the use of information technologies (IT) resources in a profitable and sustainable manner. The purpose of this study was to understand the social, individual, and contextual factors that drive actors belonging to the sector of organizations in the Latin American and Caribbean (LAC) context on the intention to adopt GIT to generate strategies that promote its application. A model resulting from the analysis and integration of adoption models was proposed, including the theory of planned behavior (TPB), norm activation theory (NAT), GIT adoption model (GITAM), and environmental sustainability variables. To determine the predictive factors, the research team collected data from organizations belonging to Mexico, Panama, Honduras, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, and Chile, where information was collected from personnel at strategic, tactical, and operational levels. Data were collected from 562 informants from different productive and service sectors and analyzed using a structural equation model with the partial least squares technique. This research provided important insights into the most significant factors that determine intent towards GIT adoption. The model analyzed contextual variables of the organization or external drivers, such as the motivating forces of environmental sustainability and environmental, organizational, and technological contexts; and behavioral variables of internal organizational actors, such as awareness of the consequences, attribution of responsibility, motivation, attitude, assignment of responsibility, and internal IT enablers.  © 2013 IEEE.

## Notas clave
- Estudio regional sobre factores que determinan la intención de adoptar TI Verde en organizaciones de América Latina y el Caribe: México, Panamá, Honduras, Colombia, Ecuador, Perú, Bolivia y Chile; 562 informantes de niveles estratégicos, tácticos y operativos.
- Modelo integrador basado en la Teoría del Comportamiento Planificado (TPB), la Teoría de Activación de Normas (NAT), el modelo GITAM y variables de sostenibilidad ambiental; analizado con ecuaciones estructurales (mínimos cuadrados parciales).
- Evidencia de que existe investigación académica regional sobre adopción de Green IT que incluye a Colombia en la muestra de datos, pero desde un enfoque organizacional/comportamental, no técnico ni de políticas públicas.
- Factores predictivos identificados: conciencia de las consecuencias, atribución de responsabilidad, motivación, actitud y facilitadores internos de TI.
- Financiado por Universidad Católica de Cuenca (Ecuador) y Universitat de les Illes Balears (España): ejemplo de colaboración regional sin liderazgo institucional colombiano.
- Uso sugerido: sección de resultados (adopción Green IT en ALC) y discusión (brecha entre intención de adopción y desarrollos técnicos o políticos en Colombia).
