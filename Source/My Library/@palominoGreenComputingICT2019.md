# Green Computing and ICT Integration in the Classroom in Rural Schools without Internet Connection

## Abstract
> This research work aims to use solar energy instead of conventional electric power for the operation of a mini-server and a virtual classroom, especially in schools where there is no electricity or in those that want to save the payment for the consumption of electric power. The system was tested by 32 classroom teachers from 4 schools in the province of Abancay in Apurímac-Perú. In each school, teachers were trained in the installation of the equipment: the mini-server and the Access Point, both devices are connected to the solar panel and work with 5V and 12V respectively. In normal climatic conditions, the equipment works continuously and without problems for 24 hours. They were also trained in the use and management of the mini-server, in the administration of the virtual classroom and in the creation of educational materials, especially for the area of mathematics. The results show that the monthly electric power consumption of the mini-server is 2.88 Kw_h and in conventional server is 67.68 Kw_h., Therefore, it has been significantly reduced in the consumption of energy_ in addition to the cost, the school would have to pay 113.35 dollars annually for electric power consumption of a conventional server and with the mini-server the cost would be zero. Finally, the teachers are satisfied with the operation of the mini-server.

## Notas clave
- **Implementación Técnica:** Sustitución de energía eléctrica convencional por energía solar para operar un mini-servidor y aula virtual en escuelas rurales de Perú (provincia de Abancay).
- **Datos Cuantitativos (Evidencia Dura):** 
    - Consumo mensual del mini-servidor: **2.88 kWh**.
    - Consumo mensual de servidor convencional: **67.68 kWh**.
    - Ahorro económico directo: Reducción de **113.35 USD anuales** a costo cero.
- **Relevancia para el Proyecto:** Demuestra la viabilidad de la infraestructura Green IT en contextos de extrema precariedad tecnológica en Latinoamérica. Provee un caso de éxito donde la eficiencia energética es la llave para la digitalización rural.
- **Uso sugerido:** Sección de Resultados (eficiencia energética extrema) y Discusión sobre infraestructura sostenible en zonas remotas de Colombia.
